# X (Twitter) — Platform Guide

## Format constraints

- **Standard post**: 280 characters (X Premium: 25,000 characters, but shorter posts perform better)
- **Threads**: Chain of connected posts; first post is the hook
- **Quote posts**: Add commentary on someone else's post
- **Polls**: Up to 4 options, 24h to 7 days

## What works for academic + business content on X

### Single posts (best for)
- Hot takes on news or market events
- One compelling stat or finding from a paper
- A question that invites debate
- A link to a new paper or blog post with a one-line teaser

### Threads (best for)
- Summarizing a research paper (5-8 posts)
- Explaining a concept step by step
- Conference takeaways
- "Here's what I learned from [experience]"

## Thread structure

A strong thread follows this pattern:

1. **Hook** (post 1): The most interesting or surprising claim. No preamble. Don't start with "Thread:" or "🧵" — just lead with the insight.
2. **Context** (post 2-3): Why this matters, what the background is
3. **Core content** (posts 3-6): The meat — findings, steps, examples
4. **So what?** (post 7-8): Implications, your take, what to watch for
5. **CTA** (final post): Question, link, or invitation to discuss

Each post in a thread should stand alone enough that someone scrolling sees a complete thought, but the thread as a whole tells a story.

## Formatting tips

- Line breaks increase readability — use them between distinct thoughts
- Numbers and stats stop the scroll: "Cap rates hit 6.2% — highest since 2019"
- Short sentences hit harder than long ones
- Use "→" or "—" to create visual structure without bullet points (bullets render poorly on X)
- Tag relevant people or organizations when genuinely relevant, not for engagement-bait

## Hashtag strategy

- 1-3 hashtags maximum (more looks spammy on X)
- Place at end of post or weave naturally into text
- Good academic/finance hashtags: #RealEstate #CRE #FinanceTwitter #AcademicTwitter #AI #PropTech
- Avoid generic tags like #Success #Motivation

## Posting cadence and timing

- Best times: Weekdays 9am-12pm CT (adjust for audience)
- 1-2 posts per day is sustainable; more is fine if content is strong
- Threads get most engagement when posted in the morning
- Engage with replies for 15-30 min after posting — the algorithm rewards early engagement

## Voice on X

X rewards personality and directness. Academic content should feel like a smart person talking at a bar, not presenting at a conference. Contractions, incomplete sentences, and rhetorical questions all work well.

**Good**: "Ran the numbers on Sun Belt multifamily. The story everyone's telling about rent growth? Not quite right. Here's what the data actually shows →"

**Bad**: "In this thread, I will discuss recent trends in Sun Belt multifamily rental markets and present findings that challenge conventional wisdom regarding rent growth trajectories."

## Common patterns for academic content

### Paper promotion
"New working paper: [Title in plain English, not the academic title]

[One-sentence summary of the finding]

[Why it matters for practitioners]

Link: [URL]"

### Conference takeaway
"Just left [session/keynote] at [conference].

Key insight: [the thing that surprised you or changed your thinking]

This matters because [connection to broader trend]"

### Market commentary
"[Stat or event] happened today.

Here's why it matters for [specific audience]:

[2-3 short lines of analysis]

Watch for [what comes next]"
