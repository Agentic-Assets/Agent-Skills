# Instagram — Platform Guide

## Format constraints

- **Caption limit**: 2,200 characters
- **First ~125 characters** show before "...more" — this is your hook
- **Stories**: 15-second clips or images, disappear after 24h
- **Reels**: Short video (up to 90 seconds), highest reach potential
- **Carousel**: Up to 10 images/slides, great for educational content

## What works for an academic + business profile on Instagram

Instagram is the most visual platform. For a finance professor and AI consultant, the goal isn't to become an influencer — it's to show the human side of the work and reach people who wouldn't find you on LinkedIn or X.

### Content that works

1. **Conference and event photos** — With a caption sharing a takeaway or reflection
2. **Teaching moments as carousels** — "5 things every investor gets wrong about cap rates" as a swipeable slideshow
3. **Behind-the-scenes** — Your desk setup, a whiteboard covered in equations, the view from a conference venue
4. **Data visualizations** — A striking chart from your research with a plain-English explanation
5. **Book or paper recommendations** — Photo of the book/paper with a mini-review caption
6. **Personal milestones** — But always tie it to a broader lesson or insight

### What doesn't work

- Text-only posts (Instagram is visual-first)
- Overly corporate or polished content (feels inauthentic on IG)
- Direct reposts from LinkedIn without visual adaptation
- Stock photos with generic captions

## Caption structure

**Hook** (first line): Grab attention before the "...more" cutoff. Lead with a question, a bold claim, or a specific detail.

**Body**: Tell the story or share the insight. Keep paragraphs to 1-2 sentences. Instagram audiences skim — make every line earn its place.

**CTA**: Ask a question or invite engagement. "What's your take?" or "Save this for later" or "Tag someone who needs to hear this."

**Hashtags**: 5-10 relevant hashtags. Can go in the caption or as a first comment.

## Hashtag strategy

Instagram hashtags are more important for discovery than on other platforms:

- Use 5-10 hashtags (the algorithm no longer penalizes more, but quality > quantity)
- Mix sizes: 2-3 large (#Finance, #RealEstate, 1M+ posts), 3-4 medium (#CREInvesting, #FinanceProfessor, 10K-500K posts), 2-3 niche (#MultifamilyAnalysis, #AcademicLife)
- Rotate hashtags — don't use the exact same set every time
- Good tags for this profile: #FinanceProf #RealEstateInvesting #CRE #AcademicLife #AIinFinance #PropTech #DataScience #ProfessorLife #MultifamilyRealEstate #SustainableFinance

## Visual best practices

Since you likely won't be hiring a graphic designer for every post:

- **Photos**: Natural lighting, not overly filtered. Authentic > polished.
- **Carousels**: Use a consistent color scheme and font. Canva templates work well for educational slides.
- **Charts/data viz**: Export at high resolution. Add a title bar so the insight is clear at a glance.
- **Text on images**: Keep it minimal — 5-7 words max on a single slide. The caption does the heavy lifting.

## Stories vs. Feed vs. Reels

- **Feed posts**: Permanent content — research insights, teaching moments, milestones
- **Stories**: Ephemeral, casual — polls ("Which paper should I read next?"), quick updates, reshares of your feed posts, behind-the-scenes moments
- **Reels**: Highest algorithmic reach. Even a simple "talking head" explaining a concept can perform well. 30-60 seconds is ideal.

## Posting cadence and timing

- 1-2 feed posts per week is sufficient for a professional account
- Stories can be more frequent (2-4 per week) since they're low-effort and ephemeral
- Best times: Monday, Wednesday, Friday 11am-1pm CT
- Post when you actually have something visual to share — forced posts look forced

## Voice on Instagram

More personal and casual than LinkedIn, but still smart. Think "the cool professor" energy — you're sharing your world, not lecturing.

**Good caption**: "Spent the morning buried in Compustat data trying to figure out why political connections matter more for some firms than others. Three cups of coffee in and I think I found it. Sometimes research is glamorous. (It's not.) 📊"

**Bad caption**: "Conducting empirical analysis on the heterogeneous effects of corporate political connections utilizing the Compustat database. #Research #Data #Academe"

## Cross-posting considerations

- **From X → Instagram**: Needs a visual. If the X post was a hot take, pair it with a relevant photo or simple graphic.
- **From LinkedIn → Instagram**: Shorten significantly. Pull out the single most interesting line and build a shorter caption around it.
- **Instagram → X/LinkedIn**: Screenshots of Instagram posts rarely work well. Rewrite for the target platform.
