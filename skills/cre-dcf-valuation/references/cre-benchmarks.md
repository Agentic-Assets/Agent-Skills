# CRE Property Type Benchmarks — Reference

## Hotel / Hospitality

Hotel valuation uses EBITDA/NOI differently; typical metrics:

| Parameter | Limited Service | Full Service | Luxury |
|-----------|----------------|--------------|--------|
| Cap Rate (NOI) | 7.0–9.5% | 6.5–8.5% | 5.5–7.5% |
| RevPAR Growth | 2–4% | 2–4% | 2–5% |
| EBITDA Margin | 30–42% | 28–38% | 35–48% |
| Management Fee | 3–5% of revenue | 3–5% of revenue | 3–6% of revenue |
| FF&E Reserve | 4–6% of revenue | 4–6% of revenue | 5–7% of revenue |
| Hold Period | 5–7 years | 5–10 years | 7–10 years |
| Levered IRR Target | 12–18% | 10–16% | 8–14% |

**Hotel-specific notes**:
- Revenue: ADR × Occupancy = RevPAR; model by ADR growth and occupancy stabilization
- NOI for hotels: Gross Revenue − Departmental Expenses − Undistributed Expenses − Fixed Charges − FF&E Reserve
- EV/EBITDA multiple approach common: 8–12x EBITDA for limited service; 10–15x for full service
- Franchise flag matters: flagged properties (Marriott, Hilton, etc.) command premiums
- Management company (3rd party vs. owner-operated) impacts NOI significantly

## Discount Rate Targets by Strategy

Discount rate in CRE DCF = targeted unlevered return (not WACC). No beta / CAPM — it is the asset-risk-based return.

| Strategy | Unlevered Discount Rate | Levered IRR Target |
|----------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Core | 5.5–7.5% | 7–10% |
| Core-Plus | 7.0–9.0% | 9–12% |
| Value-Add | 9.0–12.0% | 13–18% |
| Opportunistic | 12.0–16.0%+ | 18–25%+ |
| Development | 14.0–20.0%+ | 20–30%+ |

**Key difference from corporate finance**: CRE uses property-level risk-based discount rates derived from market cap rate expectations + risk premium, NOT CAPM/WACC with beta. Leverage (LTV) amplifies equity returns above the unlevered rate.

## Sensitivity Defaults

Use these as starting ranges for sensitivity tables if not specified:

| Axis | Default Range | Step |
|------|--------------|------|
| Exit Cap Rate | Going-in Cap ± 100 bps | 25 bps |
| Rent Growth | 0% to 5% | 1% |
| Discount Rate (Unlevered) | Base ± 150 bps | 50 bps |
| LTV | 55% to 80% | 5% |
| Interest Rate | Base ± 150 bps | 50 bps |
| Hold Period | 3 to 10 years | 1 year |
| Purchase Price | ± 15% | 5% |

Standard sensitivity outputs:
- **Table 1**: Levered IRR by Exit Cap Rate (rows) × Rent Growth (columns)
- **Table 2**: DSCR by Interest Rate (rows) × LTV (columns)
- **Table 3** (optional): Equity Multiple by Purchase Price (rows) × Hold Period (columns)

**Note on ranges**: The defaults above are broad starting points. Tighten the range to ± 50 bps / ± 1% step for stable core assets; widen to ± 200 bps for opportunistic or highly leveraged deals. Always center the table on the base-case assumption so the base cell is visually identifiable.
